Pipeline Workflow
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  [1] preprocessing.py
      │
      └──> preprocess.pkl
           │
           v
  [2] MIQP Baselines (parallel)
      ├── MIQP_linear/MIQP_global_linear.py    ──> GL results
      └── MIQP_piecewise/MIQP_piecewise.py     ──> PW results
           │
           v
  [3] DFL/scripts/generate_noisy_data.py
      │
      ├──> GL: 0-80% noise datasets
      └──> PW: 0-80% noise datasets
           │
           v
  [4] DFL Training (parallel)
      ├── run_pretraining_gl.py  ──> GL models
      └── run_pretraining_pw.py  ──> PW models
           │
           v
  [5] DFL Validation (parallel)
      ├── run_validation_gl.py   ──> GL benchmarks
      ├── run_validation_pw.py   ──> PW benchmarks
      └── run_ablation_study.py  ──> Ablation benchmarks
           │
           v
  [6] Results Generation (parallel)
      ├── results/print_tables.py        ──> LaTeX/CSV tables
      └── results/visualization.py       ──> PDF/PNG figures

Key Metrics & Output Locations
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Performance Metrics:
  • Ex-post Profit (€)       - Revenue minus costs and penalties
  • System Imbalance (€)      - Penalty for power deviations
  • Volume Violations (€)     - Penalty for reservoir violations
  • Computation Time (s)      - Wall-clock execution time

Output Locations:
  • Training data:   DFL/outputs/noisy_data/
  • Trained models:  DFL/outputs/trained_models/
  • Benchmarks:      DFL/outputs/validation_results/
  • Tables:          results/tables/
  • Figures:         results/figures/
